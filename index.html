THE ETERNAL SOUL

Jun Seba, professionally known as Nujabes, was a Japanese record producer, DJ, composer, and arranger. He was the founder of the independent label Hydeout Productions and released two studio albums during his lifetime: Metaphorical Music (2003) and Modal Soul (2005).

His music is known for a unique atmospheric blend of jazz, soul, and hip-hop. Nujabes' work continues to influence the global "lo-fi hip hop" movement, bridging the gap between technical precision and emotional resonance.
